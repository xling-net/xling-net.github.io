# WeakSim Privacy Policy

WeakSim (hereinafter referred to as “the App”) is an iOS weak-network simulator. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your data through a simple and transparent policy.

## 1. App Description and Features

WeakSim uses a system VPN (Packet Tunnel) to intercept device traffic on-device and apply network impairments, so you can reproduce subway, elevator, high-speed rail, and poor-signal conditions. Core capabilities include:

*   **On-device traffic interception**: Route device traffic through a local Packet Tunnel without modifying the app under test.
*   **Preset scenes**: Built-in conditions such as Unlimited, 2G, 3G, weak 4G / Wi-Fi, subway, elevator, high latency, packet hell, high-speed rail disconnects, and DNS timeout.
*   **Custom damage**: Delay and jitter, packet loss, upload/download throttling, duplication, reordering, tampering, periodic disconnects, and DNS faults (timeout / failure / hijack).
*   **Monitoring and replay**: Live throughput and session stats, session recordings, runtime logs, and preset scene speed tests.

## 2. Privacy Commitments

To protect your privacy, we adhere to the following guidelines:

*   **Zero User Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal identifiable information, device information, or usage habit data.
*   **No Registration or Login Required**: The App does not require an account system. You can use it without registration.
*   **Local Processing**: Simulation profiles, session recordings, and runtime logs are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or synced to iCloud.

## 3. Traffic and Local Storage

*   **Local packet processing**: While the VPN is running, the App processes network packets on-device to apply impairments. We do not collect, upload, or analyze this traffic.
*   **Local records**: Session replay data and logs remain on your device and can be cleared in the App.

## 4. Purchases

The App offers an optional Pro weekly auto-renewing subscription and a Pro lifetime purchase, handled entirely by Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). We do not process or store any payment information; all transactions are managed by Apple.

## 5. Data Security

Since all operations are executed locally, your data security is protected by your device’s operating system (iOS).
