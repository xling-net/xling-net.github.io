## 2. Description of Service

WeakSim is an iOS weak-network simulator. It intercepts on-device traffic through a system VPN and applies delay, packet loss, throttling, reordering, duplication, tampering, periodic disconnects, and DNS faults so you can test timeout, reconnect, and fallback behavior.
